AutoSkip for Streaming Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What the extension accesses

When enabled, AutoSkip runs only on the streaming domains listed in its manifest. To find controls for skipping intros/recaps and advancing episodes, it locally processes the page domain and accessible labels/attributes of playback controls. This access is necessary for the extension's main feature and may be classified as website content and browsing activity by the Chrome Web Store.

What is stored

The “Enable AutoSkip,” “Skip intros and recaps,” and “Advance to next episode” preferences are saved in Chrome's local storage. Page text and attributes are not saved. Preferences remain in the browser profile until changed or removed with the extension's data.

Service suggestions

If you enter a service name in the popup's suggestion box, the extension prepares an email and opens your default email application. AutoSkip does not send or save the suggestion automatically. You can review, edit, or discard the message. If you choose to send it, your email application and provider will process the message, which will be received at autoskipstreamings@outlook.com. Do not include personal or sensitive data.

Sharing and transmission

AutoSkip does not directly transmit data to its own or third-party servers, use analytics, sell data, or share playback-page data. The extension does not collect credentials, viewing history, titles watched, or video content. An email suggestion is transmitted only if you choose to send it using your email application.

Limited use

The access described above is used only to perform playback actions chosen by the user. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to this feature.

Third-party services

Streaming services remain subject to their own privacy practices. AutoSkip is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by those services.

Contact

Developer support: autoskipstreamings@outlook.com.

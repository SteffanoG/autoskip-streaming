# Chrome Web Store listing — English

## Name

AutoSkip for Streaming

## Summary (132 characters or fewer)

Skip intros and recaps automatically, and optionally advance to the next episode.

## Description

Stop clicking “Skip Intro” or “Next Episode” every time. AutoSkip looks for these controls on supported streaming services and performs only the actions you enable in the popup.

**You control what it does**

- Turn AutoSkip on or off.
- Choose whether to skip intros and recaps.
- Separately choose whether to advance to the next episode.
- Suggest another service: the popup prepares an email to autoskipstreamings@outlook.com for you to review and send.

AutoSkip includes service-specific rules for Disney+, Netflix, Prime Video, and Max. On Crunchyroll, it recognizes intro, recap, and credits skip controls when available. Availability depends on the title/episode and on features offered by Crunchyroll itself. Streaming services can change their interfaces, so compatibility may vary.

To find playback controls, the extension locally reads accessible labels and attributes of playback buttons on the listed domains. This page content is not stored or transmitted. Preferences stay in Chrome's local storage. AutoSkip uses no analytics and does not directly send data to its own or third-party servers. A suggestion is sent only if you choose to send the prepared message from your email app.

AutoSkip is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Disney, Netflix, Amazon, or Warner Bros. Discovery. Platform names are used only to identify intended compatibility.
